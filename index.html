Questions

Before you start

How long does it take to learn?

Most beginners can play a simple song within 3–4 weeks of practicing 15 minutes a day. Chord switching is the slowest skill to build — be patient with it.

Can I learn without a teacher?

Yes. Guitar is one of the most self-teachable instruments thanks to visual chord charts and video tutorials — both of which this guide includes throughout.

Should I use a pick?

Either works for a beginner. A pick gives brighter, more consistent strums; fingers give a softer tone. Try both during the Strumming lesson and see what feels natural.
