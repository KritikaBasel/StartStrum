Guitar Anatomy

Hover (or tap) each glowing dot to learn what it's called and what it does. You'll use this vocabulary in every lesson from here on.

HeadstockHolds the tuning pegs and anchors the strings at the top.
Tuning PegsTurn these to tighten or loosen strings, raising or lowering pitch.
NutA small grooved piece that spaces the strings at the top of the neck.
Neck & FretsThe metal strips are frets. Pressing behind one shortens the string to change its pitch.
Sound HoleProjects the vibration of the strings, making an acoustic guitar audible.
BridgeAnchors the strings at the bottom and transfers their vibration to the body.

The vocabulary you'll keep using

You don't need to memorize this list — just skim it once. It'll click into place naturally as soon as you see these parts in action during the Tuning and Chords lessons.

  • Headstock — top of the neck, holds the tuning pegs
  • Nut — the "zero fret," spaces strings at the top
  • Frets — the metal strips you press behind, not on
  • Sound hole — where an acoustic guitar's sound projects from
  • Bridge — anchors strings at the bottom of the body
