Reading Chord Diagrams & Your First Chords

This is the lesson everything else builds on. First you'll learn to read a chord diagram, then you'll meet the five chords that unlock most beginner songs.

What each part of a diagram means

● Dot

Press that string at that fret with a finger. The number inside tells you which finger to use.

○ Open circle

Play that string without pressing any fret — let it ring open.

× Cross

Don't play that string at all — mute it or skip it while strumming.

Finger numbers: 1 = index, 2 = middle, 3 = ring, 4 = pinky. Diagrams are read left to right as the thick low-E string to the thin high-E string — same order as when you look down at the guitar in playing position.
