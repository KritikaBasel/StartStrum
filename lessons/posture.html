Holding the Guitar

Good posture now prevents wrist strain later. Hover over each card below to get a closer look at what "correct" looks like.

Sitting posture

🪑🎸

Classical position

Rest the guitar's waist on your left leg (raised slightly on a footstool), neck angled up — the standard for focused practice.

🧍🎸

Casual position

Rest the guitar on your right leg, body relaxed against your torso — comfortable for strumming songs.

Standing posture

Use a guitar strap adjusted so the guitar sits at roughly the same height as when you're seated — if it hangs too low, your fretting wrist has to bend awkwardly to reach the neck.

Hand position

✋

Fretting hand (left, for right-handers)

Thumb rests behind the neck, roughly opposite your middle finger. Fingers arch over the strings and press with the fingertip, not the pad.

🤚

Strumming hand (right, for right-handers)

Wrist stays loose and slightly bent, motion comes from the wrist and forearm — not stiff fingers.

Left-handed? Everything above simply mirrors — fretting with the right hand, strumming with the left, or play a left-handed guitar.
