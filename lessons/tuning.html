Tuning the Guitar

A guitar that's out of tune makes every chord sound wrong, even when your fingers are perfect. Tune up before every practice session.

Standard tuning: E A D G B E

From the thickest string to the thinnest, standard tuning is E – A – D – G – B – E. Click each string below to hear its target pitch, then tune your matching string up or down until it matches.

Tip: Tune "up to pitch" rather than down — if a string is flat, tighten it past the target slightly and settle down into place. This keeps tension more stable.

If you don't trust your ear yet

That's completely normal starting out. A clip-on electronic tuner (attaches to the headstock and reads pitch visually) or a tuner app removes the guesswork entirely — most beginners use one for their first several months.

Practice tips

  • Tune every single time you pick up the guitar — strings drift, especially on new ones.
  • Retune after your first 10 minutes of playing; new strings stretch and go flat quickly.
  • Turn pegs in small movements. A little turn changes pitch more than you'd expect.
